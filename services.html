What We Offer

Our Services

Everything you need to take your music from concept to a polished, professional release.

01

Recording

Our main tracking room is designed for exceptional sonic capture — whether you're laying down vocals, live drums, guitar stacks, or a full orchestra. Our collection of high-end microphones, preamps, and acoustically treated spaces ensures every take sounds incredible straight off the floor.

We offer a relaxed, artist-friendly environment that supports long sessions and deep creative focus. Engineers are available throughout to dial in your sound.

Vocals Live Drums Guitar & Bass Keys & Strings Full Band
02

Mixing

A great mix brings out the best in every element of your song. Our engineers use a hybrid analog/digital workflow — combining the warmth of outboard gear with the precision of modern digital tools — to craft mixes that hit hard and translate on every system.

We offer up to three rounds of revisions per project to ensure the final mix is exactly what you envisioned.

Stem Mixing Analog Processing Spatial Audio 3 Revision Rounds
03

Mastering

Mastering is the final step before your music reaches the world. We optimize your tracks for loudness, clarity, and consistency across Spotify, Apple Music, vinyl, and CD formats. Every master is delivered at the correct loudness standard for all major streaming platforms.

Receive a DDP image for CD, 24-bit WAV for digital, and streaming-ready MP3s.

Streaming Vinyl Prep CD / DDP Dolby Atmos
04

Music Production

From concept to completed track, our in-house producers help you develop beats, melodies, arrangements, and production styles that define your sound. Collaborate closely with a dedicated producer who understands your vision and helps you push creative boundaries.

Available as standalone production or bundled with recording and mixing services.

Beat Making Arrangement Co-Writing Sound Design
05

Podcast & Voiceover

Our broadcast booth is purpose-built for crystal-clear spoken word capture. Whether you're launching a podcast, recording narration, audiobook content, or commercial voiceover, our environment and engineers ensure your voice is captured at its best.

We offer full post-production editing and noise removal upon request.

Podcast Narration Audiobooks Commercials
06

Sync & Scoring

We compose and record original scores for film, TV, advertising, and games. Our team can handle everything from spotting sessions to final deliverables — stems, stems with effects, and music-to-picture sync licensing services.

Working with directors, agencies, and brands of all sizes.

Film Scoring TV & Commercial Game Audio Licensing
