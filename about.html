Our Story

The Heart Behind
the Studio

Casa Jimmy Studios was born from a lifelong love of music and a drive to create a space where artists could thrive. What started as a home studio has grown into a fully professional recording facility — but we've never lost that intimate, artist-first spirit that defines everything we do.

Founded by Jimmy [Last Name], the studio has become a creative hub where emerging talent and established artists come to record, collaborate, and push their sound to new heights. Our philosophy is simple: great music deserves great tools and even better people.

Over the years, we've worked across genres — from hip-hop and R&B to rock, pop, jazz, and beyond. Each project teaches us something new and deepens our commitment to the craft. Whether you're recording your first song or your tenth album, you'll find a home here.

Our Studio

World-Class Equipment

We invest in gear that matters — so your recordings always sound exactly the way you hear them in your head.

Microphones

Neumann U87, AKG C414, Shure SM7B, and more — the right mic for every source.

Preamps & Outboard

API, Neve, and UA hardware for that classic analog warmth and character.

Digital Audio Workstations

Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live — we work in your preferred DAW.

Monitoring

Genelec & Yamaha NS-10 references for mixes that translate everywhere.

Backline

Marshall, Fender, and vintage amps available. Drum kit and keyboards in-house.

Plugins & Processing

Extensive UAD, Waves, and Fabfilter libraries for any sonic need.

Acoustic Treatment

All rooms are professionally designed and treated for pristine sound isolation.

Remote Sessions

Source-Connect and Zoom enabled for real-time remote direction and review.
