About Us

Our Story

A Creative
Powerhouse

Casa Jimmy Studios is a full-service creative powerhouse built for musicians, by musicians. Founded with a passion for sound, vision, and storytelling, we provide professional recording, mixing, video production, and design services all under one roof.

Whether you're an emerging artist or a seasoned performer, our mission is to help you create, elevate, and share your music with the world — authentically and professionally. Welcome to your creative home.

At CJS, we understand that making music isn't just about lyrics and chords; it's a whole new world. Achieving a great sound requires a true professional — finding the right melody, selecting the right microphone for your voice or instrument, and ensuring the production enhances the song. You can be sure we'll help you with all of that and more!

The People

Meet the Team

Experienced engineers, producers, and creators — all dedicated to making your music extraordinary.

Jimmy Gentry Photo
Jimmy Gentry
Founder & Owner • Producer • Engineer • Musician

Jimmy Gentry is the visionary founder of Casa Jimmy Studios — a musician, producer, and engineer whose passion for sound has shaped the studio into the creative powerhouse it is today. With decades of experience spanning multiple genres, Jimmy brings an unmatched depth of knowledge to every project that walks through the door.

His hands-on approach ensures that every artist who records at CJS receives the personal attention and professional expertise their music deserves. For Jimmy, it's never just about the recording — it's about capturing something real.

Frank Loyal Photo
Frank Loyal
Management • Musician • Composer • Producer • Frontman

Frank Loyal is a seasoned multi-instrumentalist and creative force in the world of rock and beyond. Primarily known for his electrifying guitar work and commanding vocal presence, Frank brings a powerful blend of talent, passion, and experience to every stage and studio session. His musical arsenal spans guitar, vocals, bass, production, engineering, composition, beat-making, and intricate musical arrangements.

As the frontman, lead vocalist, and guitarist for his flagship project Gëntry, Frank channels a raw, hard-hitting rock energy that defines his signature sound. Beyond Gëntry, he's contributed to Mustang 65, Peacemaker, Adrenal, Boogie Chillin, Traffic Jam, and Mario Maissonave — showcasing his versatility across genres and roles.

Whether he's crafting beats, arranging complex compositions, or igniting crowds on stage, Frank Loyal stands as a true architect of modern rock music — driven by authenticity, relentless creativity, and an unshakable love for the craft.

Erik Reinhold Photo
Erik Reinhold
Owner's Representative • Project Manager • Music Enthusiast

Born in Amsterdam in 1962, Erik Reinhold has been a permanent resident of Costa Rica since 1992. He is the founder and principal of Reinhold y Asociados, a respected consulting firm that specializes in representing foreign investors and managing complex development projects throughout the region. His firm is known for delivering strategic guidance and hands-on project oversight, helping international clients navigate Costa Rica's business and regulatory landscape. (www.reinholdyasociados.com)

Erik is also a long-time friend of Casa Jimmy Studios founder, Jimmy Gentry, and shares a deep passion for music. A semi-professional musician, he plays guitar and sings, performing regularly with local bands Traffic Jam and General Lee. Since 2005, Erik has been organizing weekly jam sessions — first at The Whiskey Bar & Grill and, since 2019, at Casa Jimmy Studios, where his love for music and community continues to thrive.

Angelique Sain Photo
Angelique Sain
Marketing & Social Media Manager • Content Creator • Musician

From an early age, Angelique immersed herself in music, visual arts, dance, and all forms of creative expression. Her passion for the arts has shaped her journey as both an artist and a creative professional, influencing everything she creates — from music to design and digital content.

As a musician herself, she knows just how important a good studio is to the creative process. In 2018, she came across Casa Jimmy Studios and instantly felt something different. After recording some of her own songs there, she knew it was a place worth sharing. Today, she brings that same passion into her role, helping others discover just how special the studio truly is.

Now, as the studio's Marketing & Social Media Manager, she brings her creative energy to every project — whether it's developing content, planning events, or shaping marketing strategies. To her, Casa Jimmy isn't just a studio — it's home, a place to connect, create, and feel inspired.

Our Studio

World-Class Equipment

We invest in gear that matters — so your recordings always sound exactly the way you hear them in your head.

Microphones

Neumann U87, AKG C414, Shure SM7B, and more — the right mic for every source.

Preamps & Outboard

API, Neve, and UA hardware for that classic analog warmth and character.

Digital Audio Workstations

Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live — we work in your preferred DAW.

Monitoring

Genelec & Yamaha NS-10 references for mixes that translate everywhere.

Backline

Marshall, Fender, and vintage amps available. Drum kit and keyboards in-house.

Plugins & Processing

Extensive UAD, Waves, and Fabfilter libraries for any sonic need.

Acoustic Treatment

All rooms are professionally designed and treated for pristine sound isolation.

Remote Sessions

Source-Connect and Zoom enabled for real-time remote direction and review.
